THE PHYSICAL WORLD HAS NO UNDO BUTTON.

When a CNC spindle cuts aerospace titanium or bearing bronze, a 50-micron mismatch between an Excel job card, a PDF drawing, and a 3D CAD model means a scrapped part, lost tooling, and critical launch delays.

The Problem

Manufacturing records live in spreadsheets. Engineering tolerances live in PDF blueprints. True geometry lives in 3D IGES/STEP CAD files. Engineers waste hours manually hunting for discrepancies.

Our Solution

An autonomous AI engine that simultaneously triangulates Excel, PDF drawings, and 3D CAD models. Delivers instant, mathematically grounded answers with 100% cell-level citations.

Multi-Source Grounding

HOW THE TRI-SOURCE ENGINE WORKS

Look at the right viewport: you can toggle between the 3D CAD Model, the 2D Engineering Blueprint, and the Excel Manufacturing Record. Our system cross-checks all three simultaneously.

1. Schema-Agnostic Excel Reader

Auto-detects headers even with row 4 offsets, merged banner cells, and text/numeric part keys. Extracts stock size, finished size, machine, tools, and operation times.

2. PDF Drawing & GD&T Parser

Reads nominal dimensions, tolerances, notes, and revision history. Automatically resolves unit mismatches (inch vs mm) so they aren't falsely flagged.

3. Native OpenCascade 3D Kernel

Analyzes true 3D B-Rep topology: groups cylindrical faces to count holes vs bosses, measures PCD, and computes true solid volume.
