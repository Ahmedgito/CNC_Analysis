THE TRI-SOURCE SILENT FAILURE CRISIS

In space systems, a 0.2 mm mismatch or an undetected revision disparity causes satellite deployment failures, launch delays, and multi-million dollar payload write-offs.

Tri-Source Fragmentation

Critical manufacturing parameters are split across Excel job cards, PDF technical drawings, and 3D CAD files. Engineers spend 40+ hours manually cross-checking dimensions and hole counts.

RISK: MISSED ANOMALIES

Silent Revision Drift

Workshops manufacture from stale Excel sheets (e.g. Rev A bore Ø18) while engineering releases Rev B (Ø20). Like Part 3002, silent revision desynchronization causes scrap parts.

NASA's Mars Climate Orbiter was lost due to an inch-pound to metric conversion error ($327M loss). Space parts (like Part 2282 and 3004) often mix inches and millimeters without unified validation.
