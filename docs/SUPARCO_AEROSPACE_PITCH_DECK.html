To machine a single precision satellite component, an engineer must manually cross-check three conflicting sources:

Machine assignments, tooling, stock size, and cycle times. Plagued by banner rows, merged cells, and stale revision codes.

3D CAD & PDF Blueprints

Tolerances, hole diameters, and true B-Rep geometry. Trapped in vector drawings and mathematical solids.
